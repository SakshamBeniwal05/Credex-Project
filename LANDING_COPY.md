## FAQ — 5 Real Q&As

**1. How is Credex Audit different from traditional credit scoring?**

Traditional credit scores (like FICO) are static: they don't change month-to-month, and they ignore decision-specific context like loan purpose or borrower behavior trends. Credex Audit runs a *decision-level* risk analysis that factors in your specific lending rules, recent market data, and the applicant's real-time financial signals. It's built for lenders making individual decisions, not bulk portfolio scoring.

---

**2. Do I have to use Credex Audit if I already have a credit underwriting platform?**

No. Credex Audit works alongside whatever underwriting system you use. Thousands of lenders manually review credit data in spreadsheets, email threads, or legacy systems — they're our main customer. If you want Credex Audit to feed data into your existing platform (API integration), we support that too.

---

**3. Is this a replacement for manual underwriting?**

No. Credex Audit is a *second opinion* — a risk sanity check before your team makes the final call. It's built to catch outlier risk factors your underwriters might miss (like unusual cash flow patterns or concentration risk), not to remove human judgment. The best lending teams use Audit + human review.

---

**4. How do you keep the audit data private? Do you store my loan data?**

Your audit data is encrypted in transit and processed in isolated environments. We never store raw applicant PII or use your data to train models on other lenders' platforms. You can request deletion of audit records anytime. Full privacy details are in our CCPA-compliant [Privacy Policy](link).

---

**5. How much does this cost?**

During our launch phase, the first 50 users get free unlimited audits through [Month]. After that, pricing starts at $99/month for up to 500 audits, or $0.50 per audit on a pay-as-you-go plan. We also offer custom pricing for teams running 5,000+ audits/month. [Book a call to discuss your use case](link).
