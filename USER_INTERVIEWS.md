# User Interviews

**IMPORTANT:** These must be real conversations with actual users or domain experts. Do not fabricate. Reviewers will identify generic/fake interviews immediately. Conduct 3 real 10-15 minute conversations this week.

---

## Interview Template

For each interview, document:
- **Interviewee:** Name or initials (if anonymous), role, company stage
- **Date:** When you talked to them
- **Key quotes:** 3+ direct, specific quotes (show contradictions if they exist)
- **Surprising insight:** The one thing that changed your thinking
- **Design impact:** How this conversation changed your product/positioning

## Pattern Recognition

After your 3 interviews, identify:
- **Common thread:** What all 3 mentioned (the core insight)
- **Disagreement:** Where they differed (reveals market segmentation)
- **Highest leverage insight:** The thing that most changes your roadmap

**Red flags of fake interviews (what NOT to do):**
- ❌ Generic problems that could apply to anyone
- ❌ No direct quotes; just summaries
- ❌ Everyone agrees with everything (unrealistic)
- ❌ No surprised moments or contradictions
- ❌ Quotes that sound like marketing copy, not real human speech
- ❌ Same person described twice with different details

**What real interviews look like:**
- ✓ Specific job titles, company sizes mentioned
- ✓ Weird disagreements between interviewees
- ✓ At least one interview contradicts your assumptions
- ✓ Direct quotes that sound like how humans actually talk
- ✓ The surprising insight was genuinely surprising to you
- ✓ Shows actual change to product/positioning based on learnings
